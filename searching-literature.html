Searching for and Selecting Scientific Literature

In this step, you begin searching for scientific literature related to your central research question. You will learn how to search for scientific articles using Scopus and how to identify relevant studies for your topic. The goal is to develop a search profile and create an initial list of relevant articles that can be used for your literature review.

Using GenAI in AWSI

Translating Ideas into Search Strings

If you want to use GenAI, you can use it to translate your research idea into a structured Scopus search string. The primary aim of this activity is to develop your familiarity with Scopus syntax. At the same time, it is an exercise in structuring your thinking.

Students often make predictable mistakes at this stage. Ideas are entered as full sentences. Concepts are not separated properly. AND and OR are used inconsistently. This activity is designed to slow that process down. The goal is to construct a deliberate and logically coherent search string.

The immediate objective is to produce a well structured search string. However, the broader objective is to understand how search logic works and to develop a more intentional way of working with Scopus.

In this process, GenAI can function as a coach or critical conversation partner. It can help you refine concepts, test alternative combinations, and identify gaps in your logic.

To guide this process, preset prompts are provided below. Use them as a framework to structure your interaction with GenAI and to make your search logic explicit.

Using the preset prompts

The preset prompts are designed to structure your interaction with GenAI. They are not templates for a final answer, but tools to guide your reasoning process.

You will find two types of prompts below: a Course preset prompt and an Activity preset prompt. They serve different purposes.

Practical use
1. Copy the Course preset prompt first.
2. Add the Activity preset prompt below it.
3. Insert your own research idea where indicated.
4. Use the output critically and refine your work based on the logic.
