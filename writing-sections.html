Writing Sections of the Literature Review

A literature report consists of a title, abstract, introduction, main body, discussion and reference list. In addition, there are a number of general aspects that you should note. This concerns the general structure, the coherence, the depth of content, the writing style and the references in the text. Below you will find a brief explanation of the various elements.

The links below lead to course materials related to this stage. Please read them before using the GenAI activity on this page, since the activity assumes that you are already familiar with the basic content. A basic understanding is also necessary to use GenAI appropriately and critically.

Using GenAI in AWSI

Adjusting Your Sentences to Match Your Intention

This activity helps you polish your sentences so that they accurately reflect your intended meaning and the reading experience you want to create.

It is divided into two parts. First, Grammar Check focuses on identifying and correcting clear grammatical issues at the sentence level. Second, Terminology Options allows you to explore alternative word choices to better match your intention, tone, and level of precision.

The focus is on working at a small scale. You revise one or two sentences at a time, then place them back into your paragraph and evaluate how they fit.

Rather than rewriting your work, this activity supports you by providing possible options. You decide which changes to accept and how they integrate into your text.

Using the preset prompts

The preset prompts are designed to structure your interaction with GenAI. They are not templates for a final answer, but tools to guide your reasoning process.

You will find two types of prompts below: a Course preset prompt and two Activity preset prompts (Grammar Check and Terminology Options).

Practical use
1. Copy the Course preset prompt first.
2. Add the Activity preset prompt below it.
3. Insert your own research idea where indicated.
4. Use the output critically and refine your work based on the logic.
